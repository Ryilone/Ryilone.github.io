MacroMaxx

A macro tracker for iPhone that works without internet.

The whole USDA food database lives on the phone, so search is instant and offline. It also scans barcodes and knows the menus at seven restaurant chains. It's on build 17, all installed on my own phone.

ClassPath

Your next class, and when to leave for it.

A campus app for LMU. Your schedule stays on your phone with no account. It won't show a walking time until it has a campus route it can trust, because a wrong answer is worse than none when you're heading to an exam.

Expo · React Native · TypeScript · SQLite · in progress

How I build

I build with AI coding tools, mostly Claude and Codex. I decide what an app should do and how it should work, I review what the tools write, and I test on my own devices. Nothing goes on my phone until the tests pass.

I usually build something first and learn the details after. It's how I got this far this fast, and it means I'm always going back to understand the parts I skipped. Right now that's the routing math in ClassPath.
