SpotiHUD

What's playing on Spotify, without leaving what you're doing.

  • Minecraft and macOS
  • Java, Rust, Swift
  • Built with Claude and Codex

Colors: aurora. Press T like in the app, or .

The real overlay's styles, running in your browser. The buttons work. The songs are made up, since the app gets them from Spotify.

Why I made it

Every time I wanted to know what song was playing, I had to tab out of Minecraft. So the first version was a mod. Then I wanted the same thing on my desktop, and then under the Mac's notch.

The Minecraft mod

A Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21. It signs in to Spotify with OAuth PKCE, which means there's no secret key hidden inside the mod. It draws the album art, song, progress bar and a small equalizer on the game screen, and it has a settings screen and tests for the sign-in flow and the layout.

The desktop overlay

A see-through window built with Tauri and Rust that stays on top of everything. You can drag it anywhere, press T to change colors, and press C to let clicks pass through it. On the Mac it asks the Spotify app directly through AppleScript, because Apple stopped letting other apps read the system's Now Playing info in macOS 14.4.

The notch

A native SwiftUI panel under the Mac's notch, like the Dynamic Island on an iPhone. It opens when a new song starts, and the equalizer moves with the actual music. It listens to the system audio through Core Audio and uses an FFT to split it into four bands, from bass to treble. Volume and microphone changes take over the island for a second, the way they do on a phone.

A bug I remember

The mod signed everyone out after exactly one hour. Spotify's token refresh needs the app's client ID in the request, and without it you get an error, but only once the first token expires, an hour after you log in. One-line fix. Took way longer than that to find.
